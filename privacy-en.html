Jiali Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Jiali ("the app") collects, how it is used and protected, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

To sync your calendars and share them with family, we store on our servers:

InformationSourcePurpose
Anonymous account identifierProvided by Sign in with AppleIdentify your account
NicknameEntered by youShow family members who added an event
Calendars, events and member labelsCreated by youSync across your devices and share with family you invite
Push tokenGenerated by iOS when you allow notificationsSend family activity notifications

We do not access your Apple ID email, phone number, contacts, location or photos.

2. What we don't do

  • No ads, and no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs
  • We never sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties
  • We use your data only to provide the app's features

3. Sharing

Only family members you invite to a calendar can see its events. The calendar owner can change permissions or remove members at any time, and members can leave at any time.

4. Storage and security

  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in a cloud database provided by Supabase (Tokyo, Japan), protected by row-level access control
  • Sign-in credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device

5. Your rights

  • Access and edit: view and change your nickname, calendars and events in the app at any time
  • Delete: deleting a calendar or event also deletes it from our servers
  • Delete your account: Family tab › Delete Account permanently deletes your account and personal information. Calendars you created are transferred to another family member, or deleted if there is none

6. Children

The app is designed for families and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes

We will update this page and its date if this policy changes, and notify you in the app of significant changes.
