Jiali

One shared calendar for the whole family

What it does

  • One family calendar for vaccinations, parent-teacher meetings, doctor visits, bills and everything else
  • Invite your partner and family with a code — everyone sees new events right away
  • Mark who's going, star important days and see a countdown on the home screen
  • Repeating events, reminders, widgets, and an optional Chinese lunar calendar

FAQ

How do I invite family?
Open the Family tab → choose a calendar → Invite Family, then send the code or link. After installing Jiali and signing in, your family member enters the code under Family › Join with an invite code.

Will I lose my data when I switch phones?
No. Events are stored in the cloud; sign in with the same Apple ID on your new phone.

Does it work offline?
Yes. You can view and add events offline; changes sync when you're back online.

How do I delete my account?
Family tab → Delete Account. Your account and personal information are permanently deleted.
